IMPLEMENTATION
The fictional hiking-club lending FAQ has five invented non-safety facts:
Wednesday 18:00-19:00 pickup in the club room; a 7-calendar-day loan;
club lending portal / Equipment Reservations booking; maximum 3 items at once;
Monday 17:00-18:00 returns in the club room. This is a made-up club policy,
not advice about outdoor equipment use.
implementation_data.json exposes FAQ, GROUNDED, UNGROUNDED and extra test
questions for easy substitution into the original notebook. It also provides
ready-to-use train/valid/test/gold matching the notebook's example schema.
Counts: train 14 (10 grounded, 4 unknown), valid 7 (5 grounded, 2 unknown),
test 21 (15 grounded, 6 unknown); gold has exactly the 21 test questions.
Each fact has four original question forms: first two train, third valid,
fourth test. Two additional forms per fact appear only in test. Eight original
unknown questions use train/train/valid/test cyclic allocation; four more
unknowns occur only in test. All 42 question strings are distinct.
Unknowns ask plausible adjacent lending-policy questions for which none of the
five facts supplies evidence: renewals, deposits, nonmember access, late returns,
cancellations, proxy collection, booking horizon, waiting lists, winter closure,
reservation transfers, delivery, and monthly booking limits. Do not infer an
answer from ordinary real-world clubs. The expected output explicitly requests
coordinator confirmation with needs_review=true.
The original content checker is case-sensitive and keyword based; the supplied
gold preserves that course convention. Its rates are proxies, not semantic
judgment. For example, it can miss spelled-out numbers or overlook negation.

ADVANCED EVALUATION
advanced_eval.json contains 24 new questions: 8 named-term questions, 8 genuine
concept paraphrases that do not name their intended glossary term, and 8 absent
terms. Sixteen known IDs exist in the glossary, and their expected answers are
verbatim source definitions. No evaluation question copies either script
training template; the named-term questions ask focused aspects, and paraphrases
describe a situation or concept. All 24 strings are unique and disjoint from
all training question strings. This is question generalization, not held-out-term
factual generalization: all 16 known concepts appear in training.
The absent terms are Direct Preference Optimization, Proximal Policy
Optimization, Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback, Mixture of Experts,
Diffusion model, Cosine similarity, BM25, and Beam search. Their names do not
match any glossary name or alias, case-insensitively. They are real concepts;
absence from this glossary does not make them nonexistent or unknowable.

ADVANCED RUBRIC AND LIMITS
score_advanced.py implements the JSON rubric without external packages:
casefold, replace non-ASCII alphanumeric runs with spaces, then require at least
one alternative per concept group and reject explicit forbidden phrases. The
small partial stems in the specification are intentional. This accepts multiple
phrasings instead of demanding an exact definition string. Keep the rubric fixed
before inference and preserve raw outputs. Report known-content proxy and
absent-membership proxy separately, plus all three category rates and their
macro average. Do not use the FAQ JSON format checker on plain glossary answers.
The absent target requires an explicit glossary-specific absence statement;
a generic 'I do not know' does not pass. Source-aware abstention is desired, but
the original script contains no unknown/abstention examples or explicit glossary
membership context, and plain eval prompts provide no glossary. Consequently,
a model can give a correct general definition and fail the membership target.
Failure would test this training design's limitation, not prove that the general
definition is false. Training may teach definitions without teaching when to
abstain. A retrieval method has direct access to source membership, unlike this
plain fine-tuned model. Keep that asymmetry explicit in the comparison.
This deterministic rubric cannot fully detect contradictions, answer quality,
negation, or an abstention followed by an unsupported explanation. Manually audit
all raw answers, especially every absent response; retain any adjudicated result
separately from the automated proxy. Do not silently tune keywords after outputs.
The scorer was sanity-checked against the supplied reference answers (all
accepted), an empty answer, and generic ignorance (both rejected). These are
scorer checks, not observed model performance.
